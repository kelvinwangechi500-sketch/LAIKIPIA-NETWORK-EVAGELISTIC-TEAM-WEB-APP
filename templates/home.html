Welcome to NETWORK, an evangelistic team within LUCU made up of brethren from Nairobi, Central, and every corner of the university community.

We are non-denominational, non-political, and non-profit. Together, we make space for honest questions, faithful friendship, practical service, and a life shaped by the Word of God.
